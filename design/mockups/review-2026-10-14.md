# PostUp mockups — UX gap review (2026-10-14)

Reviewed locally (`index.html`) with headless Chromium at 390x844 (`?device=phone`) and 1440x900 (`?device=desktop`), Fresh style, Bold spot-checked (home, cafe, postup, quiet-feed). Every screen was opened and every visible link/button inspected. No JS errors. Screenshots: `shots/review/<device>-<style>-<screen>.png`.

Severity: **blocker** = a real user would get stuck or lose trust; **major** = noticeably confusing/missing; **minor** = polish.

Screens that exist: home, cafe, post (+sign-in sheet), posted, postup, join (+sign-in sheet), joined, quiet (map), quiet-feed A/B, projects, project, project-new, project-update, project-attach.

---

## 1. Brand-new visitor from a share link / ad
**Works:** Opens straight into content; sign-in only at action; Apple/Google sheet is short and friendly ("Sign in so Maya knows who's coming").
**Gaps**
- **Blocker – Nothing explains what PostUp is.** No one-line "what is this", no meaning of "post up", no "how it works". Home is just a map with "Live now · 3 nearby" (`phone-fresh-home.png`). Suggest: a dismissible first-visit strip/sheet: "See who's working from cafes near you. Post up, or join someone." with 3-step how-it-works.
- **Blocker – No reason to trust strangers.** Person = initial avatar + first name. No profile, no "joined X times", no verified sign-in badge, no mutuals. Suggest: tap avatar → mini profile (photo, short bio, postup count, member since, projects).
- **Major – "Join" is undefined.** Does it mean sit together? Chat? Is it ok to just say hi? Suggest a one-liner under Join: "Let Maya know you're coming — say hi at the cafe."
- **Major – No dedicated landing for an ad visitor** (see journey 8 for share links).

## 2. Brand-new visitor, home directly, nothing live
**Works:** Feed A (`phone-fresh-quiet-feed.png`) feels alive: real faces, real projects, photos, "6 postups nearby this week", "Post up here" on every card, "You're all caught up".
**Gaps**
- **Blocker – The app doesn't decide feed vs map.** The decision was feed-first when no one's live, but home is always the map; the feed is only reachable via a "List" icon on the quiet map or the desktop "Nearby" nav. Suggest: mock home routing explicitly (live → map, none → Nearby) and add Nearby as a phone tab too.
- **Major – Phone feed has a Back button although it's a root screen**, and both "Map" links go to the quiet map. Root screens shouldn't have Back.
- **Major – Feed items look tappable but aren't**: name, cafe, project tag on feed cards don't navigate (only "Post up here" works). Suggest link person→profile, cafe→cafe, project→project.
- **Major – No location context**: "Nearby" of where? No city/area label, no way to change area. Suggest "Near Venice, LA ▾".
- **Minor – "Good things happen at cafes"** is cute but doesn't explain; pair with the explainer from journey 1.

## 3. Existing user, bored/browsing
**Works:** Projects list with Nearby / Following / Mine tabs is the right idea.
**Gaps**
- **Major – Projects tabs do nothing**; no Following/Mine states, no empty state for Mine.
- **Major – No "for you"/what's new**: no notifications/activity, no "Maya posted up again", no follow button anywhere (Following exists as a tab but you can't follow a person or project).
- **Major – Phone Projects is a root tab but shows Back.**
- **Minor – Project page "Live" row and update items aren't links.**

## 4. Existing user who wants to post up now
**Works:** Post form is very fast: nearest cafe preselected, project chip, 1h/2h/3h/4pm, optional photo. Success screen with share link + Invite friends + "Add a photo" is good.
**Gaps**
- **Blocker – "Change" cafe isn't a control**; no cafe picker/search screen exists. Picking a different cafe is half the core action.
- **Blocker – No location-permission flow** ("Nearest · 0.4 mi" assumes GPS). Need: permission prompt explainer, denied state → search/pick cafe manually.
- **Blocker – Can't edit, extend or end a postup.** After "posted" there's no "My postup" status (time left, who's joining, End early, Extend). Nothing on home shows you're live.
- **Major – Duration chips/project chips don't toggle** (no selected state change) — fine for a mock, but "None" vs project and "Until 4pm" custom time need a defined behavior.
- **Major – "Working on" free text missing** — only a project chip; the postup page shows a note ("Happy to talk ROM hacking") with no field to write it.
- **Major – Privacy of what others see** isn't shown: preview "This is what people see" (first name, cafe, until when — no exact seat/live GPS).
- **Major – project-attach "Post up" skips sign-in** (goes straight to posted) — inconsistent with post flow. Also "Earlier today" (logging a past postup) next to "Now" will confuse normal users; label it "I was here earlier".
- **Minor – "Back to map" on posted** goes to home even when you came from the feed.

## 5. Someone who wants to join
**Works:** Postup page has who, what, where, distance/walk time, who else is joining, big Join. Joined screen: "Maya's been told you're coming", Directions, Leave.
**Gaps**
- **Blocker – How do I find Maya at the cafe?** No photo of her, no "I'm by the window / wearing a red hoodie", no "I'm here" button, no messaging. Suggest: poster adds optional "find me" hint; joiner gets "I'm here" + short chat.
- **Major – No messaging at all** (can't ask "still there?", "running late").
- **Major – "Directions" goes to the cafe page**, not maps. Should open Apple/Google Maps.
- **Major – "Leave" has no confirmation** and silently returns to the postup; poster isn't shown as notified.
- **Major – Postup Share button opens "You're posted up"** (the poster's success screen) — wrong screen. Same on cafe Share.
- **Major – Can't join a postup that ended / is full / poster ended early** — no states.
- **Minor – Joined screen not reachable again** after leaving it; no "My plans" spot.

## 6. Poster receiving a joiner
- **Blocker – Entirely missing.** No notification ("Jon is joining you"), no list of joiners, no accept/decline (and the open decision instant vs approve is unresolved in the mock), no remove/block joiner, no chat.

## 7. Returning signed-in user
- **Blocker – No profile, account, settings, or sign-out anywhere.** No avatar in nav, no "My postups", history, notification settings, privacy settings, delete account.
- **Major – No signed-in vs signed-out difference** in nav; mock should show both.

## 8. Friend opening a shared postup link
- **Major – Share link (`postup.now/p/7xk2`) has no landing screen.** Needs: "Maya invited you — she's at Bean Theory till 4pm working on Star Fox 64 mod" + Join + "What's PostUp?" + app-store/web. Also states: postup already ended → "Maya's done; here's what's happening nearby".

## Edge cases & cross-cutting
- **Blocker – Trust & safety is absent:** no report, block, hide, community guidelines, or safety tips. For meeting strangers (esp. women alone), need: public-place only (cafes — good), first-name only, no live GPS, ability to post "visible to followers only", block/report on every person and postup, approval option for joins.
- **Major – Location denied / different city with no data:** no screens. Need: "Pick your area" + "No one's posted up here yet — be the first" with suggested cafes (Google Places, no fake activity).
- **Major – No loading/error/offline states** (Google card failing — which actually happened — needs a fallback).
- **Major – Desktop vs phone inconsistency:** desktop nav has Map / Nearby / Projects / Post up; phone tabs are Map / Post up / Projects (no Nearby). Desktop has a "Mockups" link in the real nav. The home "Locate" button on phone goes to the quiet map (unexpected).
- **Minor – Labels:** "Postup" as a screen title, "Earlier today", "regular", "3 this wk" need plain wording. "M 2" cluster marker is unclear.
- **Minor – Phone screens with Back on root tabs** (Projects, Nearby).
- **What brings people back:** nothing yet — no follows, no notifications ("2 people you know are posted up"), no streaks/regular-cafe identity, no weekly recap. Add at least notifications + follow.
- **Bold spot check:** visually holds up on both devices; same structural gaps (`phone-bold-home.png`, `desktop-bold-quiet-feed.png`).

## Top 10 to fix/add in mockups before development
1. **Poster side of joining**: "Jon is joining you" notification, joiner list, approve/remove, and decide instant vs approve.
2. **Meeting at the cafe**: "find me" hint, "I'm here", lightweight chat between poster and joiners.
3. **My live postup**: status on home, time left, Extend, End early, Edit.
4. **Cafe picker + location permission** (ask, denied → search/pick area manually).
5. **Trust & safety**: mini profile, report/block on people & postups, safety tips, visibility options.
6. **What-is-PostUp explainer** for first visits + share-link landing page (incl. ended-postup state).
7. **Home routing**: feed when nobody's live, map when someone is; add Nearby to phone tabs; consistent phone/desktop nav.
8. **Profile / account**: avatar in nav, my postups, settings, notifications, sign out, delete account.
9. **Fix wrong/dead links**: Share → share sheet (not "You're posted up"), Directions → maps app, feed card names/cafes/projects tappable, Projects tabs, Leave confirmation, project-attach should require sign-in, remove Back on root tabs.
10. **Empty/new-city + error states**: no-data area, ended/full postup, Google card failure, loading.
