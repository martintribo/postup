Curious visitor joins someone live

MVP mustDraft [M1024]
· open with notes

Persona: New visitor, signed out, curious; lands on / with live postups nearby.
Start: auth=out · live=live · phone · entry /
Goal: Understand the site and join someone; sign in only at Join. [M1024]

Steps

  1. #home auth=out · live=live · mode=live
  2. #postup auth=out · live=live
  3. #join-signin auth=out · live=live
  4. #joined auth=in · live=live

Branches and edge cases

  • Not now on the sheet: stays on the postup, still signed out. [drawn] [M1024]
  • Switches to Building to look at projects first. [M1018b] [M1024]

Success

  • No sign-in before an action. [FS]
  • After sign-in the original action completes. [M1018] [M1024]

Connected flows

  • Cautious joiner checks a few postups, then joins: branch out at step 4→9; merge in at step 4 (from its 9)
  • Quick joiner: branch out at step 4→3; merge in at step 4 (from its 3)
  • Share link to a live postup: branch out at step 4→3; merge in at step 3 (from its 2)
  • Curious browser: live now, then past content: branch out at step 2→2
  • Stumbled on it: what is this?: branch out at step 2→2
  • Leave a postup you joined: branch out at step 4→1

Branch out = another flow continues differently from the same site state; merge in = another flow arrives here from somewhere else. [M1025]
